Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Email address for account creation
  • Device push notification tokens
  • Location follows and preferences
  • Drop interactions (views, engagement)
  • Age verification status (21+ confirmation)
  • Loyalty program visit history and timestamps
  • Vendor analytics (follower counts, drop views, engagement metrics)

2. How We Use Information

  • Send push notifications about new drops and targeted deals
  • Restrict alcohol-related notifications to 21+ verified users
  • Segment loyalty program members for targeted vendor promotions based on visit history and recency
  • Provide personalized content and vendor analytics
  • Improve service functionality
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage

Data is stored using Supabase (PostgreSQL database). Push tokens are managed by Expo. We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase for data storage
  • Expo for push notifications
  • Stripe for payments (vendors only)

5. Data Retention

We retain data while your account is active. You may request account deletion by contacting support.

6. Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Request data deletion
  • Opt out of push notifications
  • Delete your account

7. Children's Privacy

The service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email: privacy@nommey.com
