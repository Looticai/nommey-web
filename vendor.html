Frequently asked questions

What happens if I cancel?
Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. No partial refunds, no surprises.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Yes. Upgrade anytime from nommey.app/vendor. The difference is charged immediately and your new features activate right away.
What's a drop?
A drop is a time-sensitive special — happy hour, weekend deal, or event — that gets broadcast as a push notification to everyone following your venue.
Is a credit card required for the free trial?
Yes. Stripe requires a card to start the trial, but you won't be charged anything for the first 2 months. Cancel before the trial ends and you'll never see a charge.
How do I get started?
Pick a plan below and click "Get Started." If you already have a Nommey account, enter your email. If not, you'll be prompted to create one right here — no app download required to subscribe.
What if I need help?
Email us at nommeybeta@gmail.com — we're happy to help.
